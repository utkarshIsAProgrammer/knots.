🚀 Introducing Knots: Sovereign Zero-Knowledge Anonymous Relayer

Most messenger apps hoard data behind their walls. Knots is built differently — it's a zero-knowledge, privacy-first messaging platform where you own the keys, and the server remains blind.

🔐 End-to-end encrypted by default — your messages never leave your device as plaintext. Only ciphertext travels over the network.
👁 Burn-on-read / View-Once — messages self-destruct after being read. The server never stores plaintext.
🕶 Plausible deniability (decoy mode) — enter a sterile workspace under duress. Your real vault stays hidden.
📦 Traffic-masking cryptography — fixed-size packet padding means "Hi" and a full essay look identical to observers.
🌑 Stealth offline QR handshake — exchange public keys directly between devices. No server-mediated key exchange, zero trail.
⏰ Ephemeral identities — disposable knot aliases that self-destruct after 1 hour or when the chat window closes.

Why it matters: traditional E2EE still leaves metadata trails. Knots eliminates server-side visibility entirely — from message contents to contact discovery to traffic analysis.

You own the keys. Your contacts stay yours. No platform can lock you out or ban you.
